Pause & resume

Pause. Breathe. Carry on.

Take a sip of water. Gather your thoughts. Set up the next shot. Pause your recording, then resume when you're ready. You can reframe or switch between Camera and Screen + Camera while paused; your teleprompter pauses and resumes with you.

  1. RecordLet the story begin.
  2. PauseTake your moment.
  3. ResumePick up the thread.

Made for the way you tell a story.

See it. Shape it. Save it.

Your canvas comes together live: framing, backgrounds, and camera placement are already part of the video when you stop recording.

Make the shot yours

Give yourself the whole frame, settle into a picture-in-picture, or use person cutout. Choose your canvas shape, background, and camera position.

A little help with your words

Keep your script close with the built-in teleprompter. A countdown gives you a moment to settle in; scrolling follows the pace you choose.

Bring your screen when it helps

A product walkthrough, a lesson, a work in progress. Add screen sharing whenever showing something helps you tell the story.

Your voice, your finished file

Choose your microphone and add system audio when you need it. Save a MOV video in H.264 for everyday sharing or ProRes 422 for further editing.

Made on your Mac

Your recording, script, and person cutout are processed locally. Open OneAct and start creating, with no account required.
